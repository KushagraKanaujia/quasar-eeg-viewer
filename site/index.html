Quasar EEG Viewer

Turn a raw EEG/ECG recording (CSV) into an interactive plot. You can pan, zoom, scroll through time and turn channels on and off. Brain channels are plotted in µV and heart/eye channels in mV, each on its own axis.

    How it works

    1. Load. Lines starting with # (device header lines, e.g. sample rate or headset name) are skipped, and the rest is read as a CSV that must have a Time column in seconds.
    2. Classify. Column names are matched to known channel names: 10-20 system electrodes (Fz, Cz, P3, ...) are EEG, X1:LEOG/X2:REOG are ECG/EOG, and CM is the reference. Device columns like Trigger or ADC_Status are ignored.
    3. Plot. EEG goes on the left axis in µV. ECG/EOG and reference are divided by 1000 and go on the right axis in mV, so the large and small signals are both readable.

    The viewer on this page is a JavaScript version of the same steps, so you can try it without installing Python. One difference: columns it doesn't recognise are still added, but hidden until you click them in the legend.

    Built with Plotly, Papa Parse and Pico CSS (all MIT). Sample recording: a 42.5 s, 300 Hz DSI-24 headset export included in the repo.
